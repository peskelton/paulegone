Paulégoné is the contract design and engineering practice of Paul Skelton — a 22-year industrial designer and product engineer who has worked across corporate, agency, startup, and freelance, in nearly every role on the product development chain.

The positioning is unusual on purpose: most contract designers stop at CAD. I take products through manufacturing, supplier coordination, and — for my own brands — into retail, advertising, and customer support. That arc is the work, and I know which decisions early in design will cost or save money in production three years later.

For my own brands, I use the same discipline. SoftCrutch is shipping. YumeKeki is in validation. Other product concepts are at varying stages, and the pipeline informs the contract work in both directions.
